# ADR 010: Practical HPP and operational bookkeeping

Status: Accepted for V1.

## Decision

Per-unit material, packaging, production and other costs; sale-entry current-version cost snapshots; no FIFO or full general ledger.

## Rationale

A farmer group needs understandable economics before enterprise accounting machinery.

## Consequences and change conditions

Expose estimated gross profit honestly; no automatic loss valuation or retroactive cost reconstruction.
